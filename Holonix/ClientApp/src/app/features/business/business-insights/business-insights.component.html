How to read these numbers

Booked value is accepted and legacy job value, not cash collected. Cancelled jobs are counted separately and excluded from value totals. Completion uses the recorded completion timestamp, not the acceptance status. Pending requests and expired checkout attempts are excluded.

Payments collected, outstanding balances, refunds, and actual working hours are unavailable: the current records do not contain captured/refunded amounts, balance transactions, or time entries. A successful payment status alone cannot establish net cash collected.

Employee value is split equally among the assigned team using current assignments, with remaining cents distributed in employee ID order. Unassigned work is shown separately. Employee job counts reflect participation and can overlap; dashboard totals count each job once. Employee filters select whole jobs, while employee rows show each team member’s allocated share. These figures are not wages or payments collected. Service revenue allocation is deferred. Scheduled times use business wall-clock values; completion timestamps are displayed in your local timezone.
